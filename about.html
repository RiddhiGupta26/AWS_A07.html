About Student Registration Portal

The Student Registration Portal is a simple web-based system designed to make the student registration process easier and more organized. Students can provide their personal, contact, and academic information and select the courses they are interested in.

Purpose of the Portal

The main purpose of this portal is to provide students with a simple and user-friendly registration process. It organizes different types of student information into separate sections so that registration can be completed easily.

Registration Process

  1. Create your student account.
  2. Enter your personal information.
  3. Provide your contact information.
  4. Enter your academic details.
  5. Select the courses you are interested in.
  6. Review your information and submit the form.

Portal Features

Simple

Easy-to-understand registration process.

Organized

Information is divided into clear sections.

User Friendly
